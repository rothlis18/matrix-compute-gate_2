**Algorithmic Enclosure:**

1. **Real-Time Semantic Filters:** These filters analyze data in real-time to detect and remove content that violates predefined compliance guidelines. The effectiveness of these filters is quantified by their ability to accurately identify and remove non-compliant content while minimizing false positives and negatives.

2. **Telemetry Harvesting:** Centralized systems collect and analyze telemetry data to monitor user behavior and enforce compliance. The volume of telemetry data collected is a critical factor in determining the computational resources required for analysis.

2. **Tokenized Transaction Barriers:** Pay-to-query mechanics are used to establish tokenized transaction barriers, ensuring that only authorized users can access data. The number of tokens required for access is determined by the sensitivity of the data and the level of compliance required.
