\[ R = \frac{C_{\text{local}}}{C_{\text{central}} + L} \]

\[ V_{\text{min}} = \frac{D_{\text{total}}}{T_{\text{query}}} \]

\[ P = \frac{Q_{\text{cost}}}{N_{\text{queries}}} \]

where \( P \) is the price per query, \( Q_{\text{cost}} \) is the total cost of queries, and \( N_{\text{queries}} \) is the number of queries.

\[ H_{\text{eff}} \geq \frac{D_{\text{process}}}{T_{\text{offline}}} \]
